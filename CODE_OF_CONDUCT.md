# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

GGUFBox is intended to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community for contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code constructively; do not attack people.
- Welcome differing perspectives and give feedback that helps others learn.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and sustained disruptive conduct are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, restrict, or remove participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers will review reports as fairly and confidentially as possible.
